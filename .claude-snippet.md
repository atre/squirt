## Logs
- Never read raw logs (kubectl/docker/journalctl/files) into context when they
  may exceed ~200 lines: pipe through `squirt` first (`… | squirt --level warn`,
  `squirt k8s -n <ns> deploy/<x> --since 1h`, `squirt diff before.log after.log`).
  Drill down with `--grep '<re>' --sample 5`; cap with `--tokens <n>`.
